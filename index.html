Applying

Key dates for September 2027

Applications for Nursery and P1 places are made online through the Education Authority (EA) using EA Connect. The dates are the same for Nursery and P1, apart from when you'll hear back.

  1. Wednesday 9 December 2026

    Admissions criteria for pre-schools and primary schools published on the EA website.

  2. Wednesday 6 January 2027, 12 noon

    EA Connect opens for online applications.

    Our Fun Morning on 9 January is the perfect time to visit
  3. Wednesday 20 January 2027, 12 noon

    Applications close. Make sure yours is in by this time.

  4. Thursday 18 March 2027

    Outcomes issued for P1, and for Nursery (Stage 1, target age).

  5. Friday 11 June 2027

    Nursery Stage 2 outcomes issued.

Dates are set by the Education Authority. Always check the EA website for the latest information.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I bring my child along?

Yes, please do! Our open sessions are a great chance for children to explore the classrooms and meet our teachers.

What if I can't make any of the dates?

Call us on 028 7083 2194 or email ktaylor357@c2kni.net and we'll arrange a visit at a time that suits you.

When do I apply for a Nursery or P1 place?

Applications open on the EA website at 12 noon on Wednesday 6 January 2027 and close at 12 noon on Wednesday 20 January 2027.

Do you offer care before and after school?

Yes. Breakfast Club starts at 8:05am, and our Causeway Club offers wraparound care for Nursery to P7 from 1:15pm to 5:20pm.

How do you support children with additional needs?

We're an Autism Impact School and have two Specialist Provision in Mainstream (SPiM) classes. Come and chat to us at an open session to find out more.
